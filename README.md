Agent‑Based Crisis Simulations & Digital Twin Prototypes

Multi‑Agent Systems • Crisis Response • Robotics Autonomy • Industrial Simulation

This repository contains a collection of agent‑based crisis simulations developed between 2024–2025.
They model multi‑agent behavior, human–robot interaction, safety‑critical decision making, and digital‑twin style industrial failures.

All simulations were built from scratch using Python and represent scenario‑driven prototypes for:

• Agent‑Based Modeling (ABM)
• Digital Twins / Industrial Simulation
• Robotics Autonomy & Multi‑Agent Coordination
• Safety‑Critical Systems & Ethical Decision Logic


These projects demonstrate systems thinking, agent ecology design, and crisis‑response modeling — skills used in aerospace, robotics, emergency response, industrial automation, and research labs.

---

🔥 Core Concepts Across All Sims

• Multi‑agent coordination under stress
• Human physiology modeling (heart rate, heat, panic, grief)
• Robot physiology modeling (motor temperature, thermal death, load)
• Cascading failure events (breaches, leaks, radiation, power loss)
• Resource constraints (oxygen, bandwidth, operations, sensors)
• Sacrifice logic (bots dumping power or shutting down to protect humans)
• Symbolic ontology (sparks = humans, ants = agents)
• Crisis ethics (priority classes, protective bias, triage behavior)
• Digital‑twin style facility modeling (LHC cryogenics, reactor dynamics, moon base ecology)

📦 Included Simulations

1. Moon Base Crisis Simulation

Category: Agent‑Based Modeling • Robotics Autonomy • Synthetic Ecology
Models a micrometeorite swarm hitting a lunar base.
Features:

• 500 humans (“fire ants”) with physiology
• 50 robots (“bull ants”) with thermal limits
• oxygen depletion, CO₂ rise, power loss
• triage logic, herding behavior, bot sacrifice
• grief and post‑event physiological strain


Demonstrates: multi‑agent coordination, crisis ecology, human‑robot interaction.

---

2. LHC Cryogenic Leak Simulation

Category: Digital Twins • Industrial Simulation • Safety‑Critical Systems
Simulates a cryogenic leak at the Large Hadron Collider with:

• magnet temperature drift
• radiation accumulation
• power surge modeling
• IoT sensor networks
• human wearables (bioelectric + thermal output)
• bandwidth constraints
• severity scoring
• bot sacrifice under “Super Hero Clause”


Demonstrates: digital‑twin logic, industrial crisis modeling, sensor ecosystems.

---

3. Spark‑Hero Ethics Simulation

Category: AI Safety • Robotics Autonomy • Ethical Systems
Implements a symbolic ontology:

• 🔥 sparks = humans (non‑replaceable origin)
• 🐜 ants = agents
• 🔥🐜 spark‑ants = human operators


Includes:

• snap‑action reflex
• chaos filter
• speculate‑and‑refine loop
• spark priority
• hero factor (full power dump to save humans)


Demonstrates: value hierarchies, protective bias, ethical decision logic.

---

4. Nuclear Reactor Failure Simulation

Category: Safety‑Critical Systems • Digital Twins
Models cascading failures in a reactor environment:

• coolant loss
• thermal runaway
• sensor degradation
• multi‑agent triage
• sacrifice logic for containment


Demonstrates: crisis‑response modeling, industrial safety logic.
